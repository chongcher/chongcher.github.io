(While ISC2 member policies mean I can't mention the specific certification exam here, details are available in the Credly badge on my profile if you're curious.)

This achievement has been a long time coming. I had attended the preparatory course back in July 2025 (a big thank you to Paolo Miranda and my coursemates for their insightful discussions), but work quickly took over and taking the exam kept slipping down the priority list. It wasn't until my exam voucher was about to expire that I finally committed to revising. That's when I found out my access to the textbook had expired; safe to say that wasn't part of the study plan! I definitely didn't feel prepared, but the training I received and the experience I've gained on the job proved invaluable and helped me across the finish line.

A huge thank you to Singapore Institute of Legal Education (and Tan Xi'en Rachel!) for sponsoring the course, championing continuous learning, and fostering an environment that has shaped my perspective on cybersecurity. Over the past year, I've come to appreciate that effective cybersecurity is about enabling organisations to achieve their goals safely, and not simply sticking with the status quo. Good security is built on clear policies, transparent governance, proportionate risk management, and collaboration across teams. It gives organisations the confidence to innovate rather than reasons to stand still.

This is another milestone, but there's still plenty more to learn. I'm excited to continue building my expertise and helping organisations manage cyber risk in ways that support, rather than slow down, digital transformation. The biggest lesson I've taken away is that the toughest security challenges are rarely technical. They're usually organisational, and having the opportunity to tackle them in the real world has made this achievement far more meaningful than passing an exam.
